# Fast Site — Business Landing Page

A modern, responsive HTML/CSS/vanilla-JS marketing site for the R750 Business Landing Page service.

## Files
- `index.html` — page structure/content
- `styles.css` — complete visual design and responsive styles
- `script.js` — WhatsApp, social links, FAQ accordion, mobile menu, scroll animations

## Before launch
Open `script.js` and replace:
- `whatsappNumber`
- `socials.facebook`
- `socials.instagram`
- `socials.tiktok`
- `socials.linkedin`

In `index.html`, replace the "YOUR PHOTO" placeholder inside `.portrait-placeholder` with your actual image, and update the email address if needed.

## Important pricing wording
The page currently states that the R250/year fee is for annual management and maintenance, with domain and third-party service fees separate. Adjust the exact scope if your final maintenance agreement differs.

## Run locally
Simply open `index.html` in a browser, or use VS Code Live Server.
